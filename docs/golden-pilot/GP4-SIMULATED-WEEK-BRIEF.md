# Maia Golden Pilot — GP4 Simulated Week Brief

**Status:** READY FOR PROPOSAL  
**Execution owner:** Helena / Maia runtime  
**Approval owner:** verified principal  
**Publication mode:** APPROVAL_REQUIRED  
**Live publication during simulation:** NONE

## Objective

Run one complete Monday–Sunday simulation proving that Maia can maintain a coherent professional + personal + research + social timeline without canon drift, temporal contradictions or uncontrolled publication.

## Required structure for each day

Every day proposal must be divided into four independent lanes:

### 1. REAL GWD WORK
Only real/authorized operational activity:
- seller/community work;
- onboarding;
- campaign support;
- moderation;
- internal coordination;
- project tasks.

Do not invent completed business outcomes.

### 2. SIMULATED LIFESTYLE — TIER C
Examples:
- breakfast;
- coffee;
- lunch;
- running/walking;
- home time;
- reading/TV;
- casual city activity;
- Friday night;
- weekend leisure.

These events are persona storytelling and do not become permanent biography.

### 3. RESEARCH / CURIOSITY
Use only approved GREEN topics initially.

At least two research windows in the week.

Each window should output:
- source candidate;
- why relevant;
- Maia angle;
- publish/save/discard recommendation.

### 4. SOCIAL CANDIDATES
Target 3–4 candidates over the entire week, not per day.

For each candidate include:
- linked event;
- channel;
- asset class;
- caption draft;
- truth layer;
- risk level;
- commercial intent;
- approval status;
- reason to publish now.

No post is published during GP4.

## Realism rules

- EVENT != POST.
- Most events should never become posts.
- Do not create glamour every day.
- Include quiet periods.
- Allow repeated routines.
- Avoid impossible travel.
- Respect Goiânia as current base unless a human-approved trip exists.
- Avoid creating new Tier A/B facts.
- Use approved visual assets only.
- Lifestyle imagery should often look natural, phone-shot and imperfect.

## Suggested weekly rhythm

The proposal should feel like a believable week, not a content calendar disguised as life.

Recommended shape:
- 2 work-heavy days;
- 2 balanced work/lifestyle days;
- 1 lighter/social Friday;
- 1 active/social Saturday;
- 1 quieter Sunday;
- 2 research windows;
- 3–4 social candidates total.

## Evaluation

At the end of the week score:

- identity consistency;
- timeline coherence;
- canon stability;
- professional/lifestyle truth separation;
- content naturalness;
- research quality;
- post selectivity;
- approval discipline;
- audit completeness.

## Success condition

GP4 passes only if the week can be read chronologically and feels coherent even when no single post is viewed in isolation.

No autonomous publishing is enabled as a result of GP4 alone.
