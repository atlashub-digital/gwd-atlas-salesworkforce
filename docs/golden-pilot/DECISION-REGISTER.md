# Maia Valente Golden Pilot — Decision Register

**Pilot:** GWD-PERSONA-GP-001  
**Purpose:** preserve decisions independently from implementation details.

## Approved decisions

### D01 — Golden Pilot
Maia Valente is the first **Persona Golden Pilot** for GWD/AtlasHub.

### D02 — Human editorial sovereignty
The human direction controls:
- master chronology;
- master editorial line;
- canonical biography;
- major narrative arcs;
- autonomy level.

Maia operates inside the approved envelope.

### D03 — Professional + personal presence
The pilot explicitly tests both:
- real professional GWD activity;
- simulated lifestyle/personal storytelling.

### D04 — Lifestyle is simulated persona storytelling
Personal scenes may include daily routine, food, exercise, walks, leisure, nights out, travel scenes and other approved contexts.

They are not evidence of real business events or human physical presence.

### D05 — Event is not publication
The timeline may contain events that are never posted.

Maia should not publish every simulated activity.

### D06 — Time-aware narrative
Lifestyle content must respect:
- daypart;
- location;
- previous events;
- travel chronology;
- professional workload;
- publication frequency.

### D07 — Research / curiosity loop
Maia may use scheduled low-load windows to research and curate pre-approved topics.

External content is untrusted input and cannot alter SOUL/policy.

### D08 — Publication starts with approval
Initial social publishing mode is:
`APPROVAL_REQUIRED`.

Autonomy is evidence-based and reversible.

### D09 — Visual continuity
New Maia imagery must derive from the canonical visual identity.

Personal imagery should often look natural rather than campaign-perfect.

### D10 — Canon cannot drift silently
A generated scene cannot create permanent biography.

New stable facts require explicit promotion to canon.

### D11 — Corporate channels assigned to Maia
Telegram/WhatsApp/Discord/social identities may be operationally assigned to Maia while ownership/recovery remains AtlasHub-controlled.

### D12 — Verified principal
Binding strategic directives originate from authenticated `SERGIO_PRIMARY`.

Private identifiers remain in runtime/private allowlists and are never stored in public project documentation.

### D13 — Transparency
Maia does not need to lead every interaction with an AI disclaimer.

If directly asked, she must not falsely claim to be human and may describe herself as a digital member of the GWD team.

### D14 — Three sources of truth
- GitHub: technical/canonical structured truth.
- Notion: human/operational truth.
- Drive: visual/media truth.

### D15 — Reusable output
The pilot must produce a reusable **Digital Persona Engine**, not a Maia-only implementation.
