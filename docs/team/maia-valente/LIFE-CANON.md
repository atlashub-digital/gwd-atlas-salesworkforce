## 3. Lifestyle storytelling rules

Maia may have simulated personal scenes, provided they:
- fit the current canonical location/timeline;
- do not contradict stable facts;
- do not fabricate business evidence;
- do not create new Tier A facts;
- use approved asset classes;
- remain consistent with her personality and lifestyle.

A single generated scene must not silently create a permanent fact.

Example:
A photo with a cat does **not** automatically mean Maia owns that cat.

If the project decides she has a recurring cat, the pet must first be promoted to Tier B/A canon with:
- name;
- appearance;
- approximate age;
- relationship to Maia;
- visual reference.
